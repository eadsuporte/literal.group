Case study | Correctional environment

An LMS for security institutions with complete isolation

We built a learning environment for use inside a correctional facility, with no communication between learners and no access from outside the unit - including administration or assessment marking.

No internet No learner communication Management only inside the unit
🏢

The setting

A correctional facility needed digital learning without creating any communication channel or contact with the outside environment.

🔒

The central rule

No one outside the facility can access the system. Administration, learning support and marking all happen locally.

📖

The outcome

Learners could study individually in a controlled and auditable environment without social tools or communication between accounts.

⛔ Non-negotiable project rule

No person outside the correctional facility can access the LMS

There is no remote administration, external teacher access, connected support or internet-based assessment marking. Every administrative and learning action must happen physically inside the unit, on authorised equipment connected only to the internal network.

The real problem

Deliver digital education without opening a communication channel

A conventional LMS is designed to be connected and usually includes messaging, forums, profiles, participant lists, notifications and multiple forms of interaction. In a correctional environment, those features may conflict with institutional security rules.

The challenge was to retain what matters for learning - content, activities, assessments, tracking and records - while removing anything that could allow contact between learners or communication with people outside the unit.

Environment boundaries
  • No internet or cloud access
  • No external administrator login
  • No assessment marking outside the unit
  • No messaging, chat, forums or comments
  • No visibility of other learners
  • No links or resources loaded from external servers
The delivered solution

An LMS reduced to the essentials: learn, assess and track

The platform was organised as an individual local learning environment with restricted permissions and communication features removed or unavailable.

🏠

Infrastructure inside the unit

The application, database, files and content remain hosted within the facility's authorised infrastructure.

👤

Individual experience

Each learner can access only their own courses, activities and results, without seeing peers or contact mechanisms.

📝

Internal assessment

Automatically graded assessments can be processed locally. Work requiring human review is marked only by authorised staff inside the unit.

🚫

Social tools disabled

Messaging, forums, chat, comments, participant lists and other interaction points are not part of the learner experience.

🔐

Minimum permissions

Roles and capabilities were restricted so each user can perform only the actions required by their function.

📋

Local audit trail

Access, attempts, grades and administrative actions remain recorded internally for the institution's own audit process.

Security boundary

The entire workflow begins and ends inside the facility

No remote team operates the environment. The model keeps responsibility within the institutional perimeter.

01

Local administration

Accounts, enrolments, courses and permissions are managed only from authorised internal workstations.

02

Individual learning

The learner accesses content without communicating, sharing information or seeing the activity of other learners.

03

Local assessments

Assessment attempts are completed and stored in the LMS installed inside the unit.

04

Marking inside the unit

When an activity requires human review, the responsible team must use the facility's internal environment.

Results

Digital education compatible with a high-security environment

The case shows that the learning structure of an LMS can be used without bringing the risks of a social or connected platform into the unit.

📘

Focus only on learning

The learner interface was centred on content and activities without distractions or parallel communication channels.

🧱

Isolation preserved

Operation does not depend on accounts, services, libraries or people located outside the facility.

👮

Institutional control

The unit retains control over users, permissions, assessments and operational records.

🔎

Auditable actions

Relevant activities remain recorded locally for tracking and verification by authorised staff.

What this case proves

The LMS can adapt to the institution's rules, not the other way around

Correctional facilities, custody units, restricted training centres and other security institutions can deliver digital learning with local infrastructure, blocked communication and fully internal management.
